# nvgd — AD104 rendering and native video validation, 2026-11-27

Hardware tested: RTX 4060 Ti AD104 PCI `10de:2788`, CachyOS kernel
`7.3.6-1-cachyos`, nvgd `1.0.0-23`. All changes are in the private nvgd forks.
Native graphics remain nvc0/NAK and NVK/NAK; video uses Gallium/DRM NVDEC/NVENC.

## Rendering

The supplied photo shows black blocks over desktop icon text. Its downloaded
carrier SHA-256 is
`0222b3cc5d7c055d2965912cfbdee86fecac821b88d18c3295fee2c0e6f73caf`.
The photo alone does not identify a faulting method.

Source observation: `u_default_clear_texture` calls clear callbacks with a plain
`pipe_surface` template. Commit `78be4da50fd` replaced construction of a private
`nv50_surface` with a cast in both nvc0 clear callbacks. Accessing its private
width/height/offset/depth fields therefore reads beyond the template. Restore
`nvc0_miptree_surface_new` and destroy the temporary surface on every exit.
This is the same conversion used by the working GA107 source at `3e604079149`.

The image comparator allows up to two channel values of rounding difference;

## Concurrent contexts

Additional testing of the optimized package found intermittent black readbacks.
With multi-context deferral enabled, the two-context test failed in 5/5 runs;
with `NVGD_DEFER_KICK=0`, it passed in 5/5. Both contexts share a hardware channel.
Restore the context-count guard from local commit `5c6ac88c4cd`: retain batching
with one context and submit complete operations when multiple contexts exist.
Do not disable NAK, native OpenGL, GSP, or single-context batching.

After this change, both the two-context and create/destroy stress tests passed
in 10/10 runs each on AD104. The latter creates/destroys 150 contexts per run.
These are bounded stress results, not proof that every possible race is absent.

## Native decode

Select `NVC9B0_VIDEO_DECODER` when the DRM channel advertises it, including the
method-zero selector; preserve GA107's existing selectors. The 49 method/enum
definitions actually referenced by submission match the existing C5B0/C7B0 and
C9B0 headers. Header equality alone does not prove firmware-layout compatibility.

## Native encode

The previously pending Mesa -8 class-selection change is now exercised on AD104.
H.264 and HEVC 1280x720 at 90 frames both encode and strictly decode successfully.
H.264 Constrained Baseline, Main and High at 1920x1080, 30 frames each, and
HEVC Main10 at 1280x720, 30 frames, also strictly decode and report the requested
profile/dimensions. Packed-header warnings from FFmpeg remain observable.

Correct the loader's PCI/chipset namespace mismatch: its input is PCI `0x2788`,
not nouveau chipset `0x194`. The 24 admitted PCI IDs now match `nvenc_oclass`.
Tests preserve explicit overrides, other vendors and graphics-only routing.
The 24 NVENC method definitions used by submission match C7B7/C9B7.

The legacy CPU oracle initially reports 0/52 raw byte-identical configurations.
Every difference is byte `0x40b`: oracle `0x02`, current fork `0x00`. This is the
intentional deblocking change in `d6005649348`, not a new Ada regression. Do not
change the encoder or suppress the comparison to manufacture byte identity.
